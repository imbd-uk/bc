      Tips

      Set up right, train better.

      Phone placement

      • Chest height, 2–3 m away. Lean it on something steady.
      • Shoulders, elbows, hands and hips must all be in frame.
      • About 45° to the side works better than straight on, especially for jabs.
      • Light in front of you, not behind you.

      Getting accurate calls

      • Run Teach the AI once from where you'll train.
      • Snap each punch out and back. Slow pushes don't count.
      • If one punch keeps getting mixed up, redo just that punch.
      • Moved the phone? Teach again. It only takes two minutes.

      The six punches

      Jab
      Lead hand, straight out from the chin. Fast, turn the fist over at the end.
      Cross
      Rear hand, straight. Pivot the back foot and turn the hips through.
      Lead hook
      Lead hand, elbow bent about 90°, arm level with the floor. Pivot on the lead foot and turn the body.
      Rear hook
      Same shape with the rear hand. Drive the back hip through, like a cross that curves.
      Lead uppercut
      Lead hand, short and upward, palm facing you. Dip onto the lead side and drive up.
      Rear uppercut
      Rear hand, upward. Dip slightly, then drive up off the back foot.

      Orthodox: left hand leads. Southpaw: right hand leads.

      Stay safe

      • Warm up for 5 minutes before you throw anything hard.
      • Don't lock your elbows out at full speed.
      • Keep the space around you clear.
